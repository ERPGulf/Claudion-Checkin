## Other functionality inspected

Salary advance, trips, notifications, profile, QR, Home totals, clock and essential attendance status have no corresponding capability in the discovered payload. They are not assigned a guessed feature key. Backend mapping for salary advance/trips remains **Unverified**. A disabled optional history screen does not disable essential employee status reconciliation or positive evidence for an already recorded punch. Whole-app failure behavior and tenant boundaries are detailed in `02-CAPABILITY_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
